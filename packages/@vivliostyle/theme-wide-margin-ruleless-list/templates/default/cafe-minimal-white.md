## Where

Nine Cobblewright Row, Fensgate. Twenty-two seats inside, six at the counter, one long table kept for whoever arrives alone.

## House Notes

The kitchen closes an hour before the room does. We pour to order, we do not take bookings under six, and the last filter goes on thirty minutes before the shutter. Oat, soy and Jersey milk carry no surcharge; syrups are made here and listed at the counter.
