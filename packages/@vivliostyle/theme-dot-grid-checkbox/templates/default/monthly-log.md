# DAYFIELD

## The Month

- 01 — Notebook opened. Key written on the first page, in ink, once.
- 03 — Marlowe & Fen agree the commission: forty pages, hand-set, no rush.
- 06 — First frost on the cold frame. Earlier than last year by a fortnight.
- 08 — Letter to Marlowe & Fen posted. Seed ordered for the north bed.
- 09 — Ashgrove Bindery quote eleven days for the linen.
- 10 — Early train to Pentlebridge. Bindery in the morning, river path after.
- 14 — Proofs expected. Clear the table the evening before.
- 19 — Proofs back to Marlowe & Fen, corrected, by the four o'clock post.
- 25 — Bulbs in, if the ground is workable and the wind has dropped.
- 31 — Month closed. Move what is left, or strike it out.
