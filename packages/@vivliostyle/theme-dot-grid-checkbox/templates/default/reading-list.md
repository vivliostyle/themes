## Notes

- Two started at once is one abandoned. Finish, or strike it out and say so.
- _A Grammar of Hedges_ is barely about hedges. It is about the people who keep
  them, and it is the better book for it.
- _The Quiet Meridian_ turns at part three and I am not ready for it, so it
  waits on the shelf by the window.
- The last three came off the Saturday stall for the price of the bus fare.
- Struck out this month: one novel, forty pages in, going nowhere. No shame in
  the strike; the shame is in carrying it to December.

## Lent Out

- _The Ferry at Low Water_ — to the house at the end of the lane, 12 SEP. Ask
  again, kindly, and only once more.
- Nothing else leaves the shelf until that one is back on it.
